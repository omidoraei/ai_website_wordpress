FAQ

Questions we get every week

Cannot find your answer? Our team replies the same day.

What do we need to get started?
A product owner, access to a representative dataset and one engineer. We run the first workshop inside your workspace, so nothing leaves your environment.
Which models can Aurora orchestrate?
Aurora is model-agnostic. Route between OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Mistral and your own fine-tunes per step, and switch providers without touching application code.
How do you handle data privacy?
Data can stay in the EU or the US, is encrypted in transit and at rest, and PII is redacted before it reaches a model. We are SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR ready.
Can we self-host?
Yes. The Enterprise plan ships as a container image you can run inside your own VPC, with the control plane either managed or fully air-gapped.
What does onboarding cost?
Growth includes a guided 30-day onboarding. Enterprise engagements include a named success manager and a quarterly review of your agent portfolio.
Is there a long-term contract?
Monthly plans are month-to-month. Annual agreements include a discount and a commercial SLA with committed uptime and response times.
